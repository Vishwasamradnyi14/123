To My Chingu

It all began on a quiet day,
First bench, a new city, a world so strange.
New faces, unfamiliar roads,
A heart searching for a place to belong.

Then you smiled and asked my name,
A simple moment that changed everything.
You called me "Mangu,"
And I called you "Chingu,"
A friendship was born without us even knowing.

From making notes together
To making hostel lists together,
From endless Snap streaks,
To Instagram reels that said, "This is so us."

From discussing engineering colleges,
To helping each other find internships,
From meeting every single day,
To waiting for messages and calls.

Life has changed.
Our paths have become busier,
Our conversations sometimes shorter,
Our meetings fewer.

But whenever I see you,
Time quietly turns back.
For a moment,
We're still those two girls from 11th and 12th,
Laughing without worries,
Dreaming without limits.

Thank you for staying,
For listening, even when you're busy.
For understanding my silence better than my words.
For making time for me,
Even while you're driving through life.

I don't know where the future will take us,
But I know one thing —
Some friendships aren't measured by distance or time.
They're measured by the comfort of knowing
That no matter how much life changes...

You'll always be my Chingu,
And I'll always be your Mangu. ❤️
